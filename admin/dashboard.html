📸 Where to Add Photos & Videos:
Use the fields below to add media URLs. They will appear in the Media Gallery section on the season page.
📸 How to get DIRECT Google Photos URLs:

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Share links like photos.app.goo.gl/... will NOT work as images!
They will appear as clickable cards instead. To embed images, you need DIRECT image URLs.

Method 1 (Easiest - Recommended):
1. Open Google Photos in your browser (photos.google.com)
2. Click on the photo you want to use
3. Right-click on the photo → "Copy image address" (or "Copy image link")
4. Paste the URL here (should start with lh3.googleusercontent.com)

Method 2 (From Share Link):
1. Share the photo → "Create link" (you'll get a link like photos.app.goo.gl/...)
2. Open that share link in a new browser tab
3. Right-click on the photo in that page → "Copy image address"
4. Paste the direct URL here (starts with lh3.googleusercontent.com)

Method 3 (Alternative Hosting):
• Use any direct image URL from Imgur, Dropbox, or other image hosting
• URL should be a direct link to the image file
Accepted formats:
• Full URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
• Short URL: https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID
• Video ID only: VIDEO_ID
